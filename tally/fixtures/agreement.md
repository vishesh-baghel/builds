# Brightline Mechanical: Comfort Plan Service Agreement

Brightline Mechanical is an invented firm. This agreement is a synthetic fixture.

## 1. Parties and term

1.1 This agreement is between Brightline Mechanical ("the contractor") and the customer named on each work order ("the customer"), for the heating and cooling equipment at the service address on that work order.

1.2 The agreement runs for twelve months from the enrolment date and renews for further twelve month periods unless either party gives thirty days' written notice.

1.3 The plan fee is charged annually in advance. It is separate from, and never appears on, a work order invoice.

## 2. Maintenance visits

2.1 The plan includes two scheduled maintenance visits per year, one before the cooling season and one before the heating season.

2.2 The following work is covered by the plan and is not billed, whether it is done at a scheduled maintenance visit or during a repair visit: air filter replacement with a standard one inch filter; cleaning of the evaporator and condenser coils; clearing of the condensate drain line, pan and trap; and calibration or re-programming of an existing thermostat.

2.3 Covered work is covered in full, parts and labour, however long it takes and however difficult it is.

## 3. Repairs

3.1 Run capacitors, start capacitors, dual run capacitors and contactors are covered parts. Their replacement, parts and labour, is not billed, including where the part had failed completely.

3.2 Major components are not covered and are billed at the rate card price when replaced: compressors, refrigerant line sets, blower motors, thermostats (replacement of the unit, as distinct from calibrating an existing one under 2.2), and hot surface igniters.

3.3 Refrigerant added to the system is billed per pound at the rate card price, whatever the reason it was needed.

3.4 Inspecting, testing or diagnosing a component is not replacing it. A component is billed under 3.2 only when it was actually replaced.

3.5 Quotes and recommendations for future work are free. Nothing quoted is billed until the work is done.

## 4. Labour and attendance

4.1 Each repair visit carries one service call charge. The service call covers the first technician for the first two hours on site.

4.2 Each additional technician who attends the visit is billed at the additional technician rate, per technician per visit.

4.3 Time on site beyond the first two hours is billed per hour at the labour rate.

4.4 A visit that starts after 6pm on a weekday, or at any time on a Saturday or Sunday, carries the after-hours surcharge.

## 5. Work that is never billed

5.1 Travel time to and from the service address, however long the drive.

5.2 Time spent waiting for access, for the customer to arrive, for a gate code, or for a building manager.

5.3 Time spent explaining the equipment, answering the customer's questions, giving advice or discussing options.

5.4 Return visits for the same fault within thirty days of a repair ("callbacks"). No service call, labour or additional technician charge applies to a callback. Parts newly replaced on a callback are still billed under 3.2 and 3.3.

5.5 Site protection and clean up, including drop cloths, shoe covers, sweeping and vacuuming.

## 6. Replaced equipment

6.1 Removal and disposal of replaced major components is billed once per visit at the rate card price. Disposal of filters and small covered parts is not billed.

## 7. Invoicing and payment

7.1 Each visit is invoiced separately. The invoice lists each billable item with its rate card line.

7.2 Invoices are payable within thirty days. Work that was done but left off an invoice may be billed on a supplementary invoice within ninety days of the visit.

7.3 Each invoice line must describe work that the technician's completion note records as done. A line that the note does not support is not billed.

## 8. Access and safety

8.1 The customer provides safe access to the equipment. The contractor may decline work that cannot be done safely and is not liable for delay caused by lack of access.

8.2 Where refrigerant is handled, the contractor follows applicable handling and recovery regulations.

## 9. Liability and warranty

9.1 Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty. Labour carries a thirty day workmanship warranty, which is the basis of the callback rule in 5.4.

9.2 The contractor's liability for any visit is limited to the value of that visit's invoice.

## 10. Termination

10.1 Either party may end the agreement with thirty days' written notice. Work done before the end date is billed under the terms in force when it was done.
